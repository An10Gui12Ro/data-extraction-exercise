DECLARATION OF REVOCABLE LIVING TRUST

THE MARTINEZ FAMILY REVOCABLE LIVING TRUST

This Declaration of Trust is made and entered into on the 8th day of July, 2021, by and between ANGELA PATRICIA MARTINEZ (hereinafter referred to as "Grantor" and "Trustee").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Grantor desires to create a revocable living trust for the management and disposition of certain assets during her lifetime and upon her death; and

WHEREAS, the Grantor desires to serve as the initial Trustee of this Trust;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Grantor hereby declares and agrees as follows:

ARTICLE I — CREATION OF TRUST

The Grantor hereby creates and establishes the MARTINEZ FAMILY REVOCABLE LIVING TRUST (hereinafter referred to as the "Trust"). The Grantor hereby transfers, assigns, and conveys to the Trustee the property described in Schedule A attached hereto and made a part hereof, together with any additional property that may hereafter be transferred to the Trust.

ARTICLE II — TRUST PROPERTY DURING GRANTOR'S LIFETIME

During the lifetime of the Grantor, the Trustee shall hold, manage, invest, and reinvest the trust property, and shall collect the income therefrom, and shall pay to or apply for the benefit of the Grantor, such amounts of income and principal as the Grantor may from time to time request.

ARTICLE III — REVOCATION AND AMENDMENT

The Grantor reserves the right, at any time during her lifetime, to revoke this Trust in whole or in part, or to amend any of its terms and provisions, by a written instrument signed by the Grantor and delivered to the Trustee.

ARTICLE IV — SUCCESSOR TRUSTEE

In the event the Grantor is unable or unwilling to continue to serve as Trustee, the Grantor appoints her daughter, MARIA ELENA MARTINEZ, as Successor Trustee. If Maria Elena Martinez is unable or unwilling to serve, then FIRST NATIONAL BANK OF TEXAS shall serve as Successor Trustee.

ARTICLE V — DISTRIBUTIONS UPON DEATH

Upon the death of the Grantor, the Trustee shall distribute the remaining trust property as follows:

Section 5.1. To MARIA ELENA MARTINEZ, the Grantor's daughter: the residential property described in Schedule A, along with all personal effects and household furnishings contained therein.

Section 5.2. To CARLOS ANTONIO MARTINEZ, the Grantor's son: all financial accounts described in Schedule A, including any accumulated interest or dividends.

Section 5.3. Any remaining trust property not specifically disposed of herein shall be distributed in equal shares to Maria Elena Martinez and Carlos Antonio Martinez.

ARTICLE VI — TRUST ADMINISTRATION

Section 6.1. The Trustee shall not be required to furnish bond or other security in any jurisdiction.

Section 6.2. The Trustee shall have full power and authority to manage, control, sell, convey, exchange, lease, mortgage, or otherwise deal with and dispose of any property in the Trust.

ARTICLE VII — SPENDTHRIFT PROVISION

No interest in the principal or income of this Trust shall be anticipated, assigned, encumbered, or subject to any creditor's claim or to legal process prior to its actual receipt by the beneficiary.

ARTICLE VIII — GOVERNING LAW

This Trust shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas.

ARTICLE IX — SEVERABILITY

If any provision of this Trust is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Grantor and Trustee has executed this Declaration of Trust on the date first above written.
