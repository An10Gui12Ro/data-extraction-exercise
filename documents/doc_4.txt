THE CHEN FAMILY IRREVOCABLE TRUST AGREEMENT

This Trust Agreement is made and entered into on September 22, 2023, by and between HENRY WEI CHEN and MARGARET LI CHEN (collectively referred to herein as the "Grantors"), and MARGARET LI CHEN (hereinafter referred to as the "Trustee").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Grantors desire to create an irrevocable trust for the benefit of their children and descendants, and to provide for the orderly management, investment, and distribution of certain assets; and

WHEREAS, Margaret Li Chen has agreed to serve as the initial Trustee of this Trust;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the Grantors hereby create, establish, and fund the CHEN FAMILY IRREVOCABLE TRUST (hereinafter referred to as the "Trust") upon the following terms and conditions:

ARTICLE I — TRUST PROPERTY

The Grantors hereby irrevocably transfer, assign, and convey to the Trustee the property described in Schedule A attached hereto, to be held, administered, and distributed by the Trustee in accordance with the terms of this Trust Agreement.

ARTICLE II — IRREVOCABILITY

This Trust is irrevocable. The Grantors shall have no right to alter, amend, revoke, or terminate this Trust or any of its provisions, except as specifically provided herein regarding the Trust Protector's authority.

ARTICLE III — TRUST ADMINISTRATION

Section 3.1. The Trustee shall hold, manage, invest, and reinvest the trust property in a prudent manner, in accordance with the Uniform Prudent Investor Act as adopted in the State of Washington.

Section 3.2. The Trustee shall collect all income from the trust property and, after payment of all proper trust expenses, shall distribute or accumulate such income as provided in Article V.

Section 3.3. The Trustee shall keep accurate records of all transactions and shall render annual accountings to the beneficiaries.

ARTICLE IV — TRUSTEE PROVISIONS

Section 4.1. Initial Trustee. Margaret Li Chen shall serve as the initial Trustee of this Trust. The initial Trustee shall serve without compensation but shall be reimbursed for all reasonable expenses incurred in connection with the administration of the Trust.

Section 4.2. First Successor Trustee. In the event that Margaret Li Chen is unable or unwilling to continue to serve as Trustee, by reason of death, incapacity, resignation, or removal, David Wei Chen, son of the Grantors, shall serve as First Successor Trustee.

Section 4.3. Second Successor Trustee. In the event that David Wei Chen is unable or unwilling to serve as Trustee, PACIFIC TRUST COMPANY, a Washington state-chartered trust company, shall serve as Second Successor Trustee. Pacific Trust Company shall be entitled to receive reasonable compensation for its services in accordance with its published fee schedule.

Section 4.4. Trust Protector. The Grantors hereby appoint Attorney Lisa Mei Park as Trust Protector. The Trust Protector shall have the authority to: (a) remove and replace any acting Trustee; (b) modify the administrative provisions of this Trust to achieve favorable tax treatment or to respond to changes in applicable law; (c) add or remove beneficiaries, other than the Trust Protector herself or her creditors; and (d) change the situs and governing law of this Trust. The Trust Protector shall serve without compensation. In the event Attorney Lisa Mei Park is unable or unwilling to serve, the Grantors' eldest child who is not then serving as Trustee shall serve as successor Trust Protector.

ARTICLE V — DISTRIBUTIONS TO BENEFICIARIES

Section 5.1. The Trustee may distribute to or for the benefit of the Grantors' children — David Wei Chen, Sarah Lin Chen, and Emily Jade Chen — such amounts of income and principal as the Trustee, in the Trustee's sole and absolute discretion, deems appropriate for each child's health, education, maintenance, and support.

Section 5.2. Upon the youngest child reaching the age of thirty (30), the Trustee shall distribute the remaining trust property in equal shares to the then-living children of the Grantors. If any child has predeceased, such child's share shall pass to his or her descendants, per stirpes.

ARTICLE VI — POWERS OF TRUSTEE

The Trustee shall have all powers granted to trustees under the laws of the State of Washington, including but not limited to the power to:

(a) Purchase, sell, exchange, lease, or otherwise acquire or dispose of any trust property;
(b) Borrow money and encumber trust property as security;
(c) Invest and reinvest trust funds in any property deemed advisable by the Trustee;
(d) Employ attorneys, accountants, financial advisors, and other professionals;
(e) Make distributions in cash or in kind;
(f) Establish reserves for anticipated expenses;
(g) Execute and deliver any instruments necessary to carry out the purposes of this Trust.

ARTICLE VII — SPENDTHRIFT PROVISION

No interest in the income or principal of this Trust shall be subject to the claims of any creditor of any beneficiary, nor shall any beneficiary have the power to anticipate, alienate, dispose of, pledge, or encumber any such interest. No interest of any beneficiary hereunder shall be subject to attachment, garnishment, execution, or any other legal or equitable process.

ARTICLE VIII — TAX PROVISIONS

Section 8.1. Notwithstanding any other provision of this Trust, no distribution shall be made that would discharge the legal obligation of any person to support a beneficiary.

Section 8.2. It is the intent of the Grantors that transfers to this Trust qualify for the federal gift tax annual exclusion under Internal Revenue Code Section 2503(b).

ARTICLE IX — GOVERNING LAW

This Trust Agreement and the administration of this Trust shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Washington.

ARTICLE X — SEVERABILITY

If any provision of this Trust is declared invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Grantors and Trustee have executed this Trust Agreement on the date first above written.
