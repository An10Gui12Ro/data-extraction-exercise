LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT OF JOHN MICHAEL SMITH

I, John Michael Smith, a resident of Springfield, County of Sangamon, State of Illinois, being of sound mind and disposing memory, and not acting under duress, menace, fraud, or undue influence of any person whomsoever, do hereby make, publish, and declare this instrument to be my Last Will and Testament, hereby revoking all former Wills and Codicils heretofore made by me.

ARTICLE I — FAMILY DECLARATIONS

I declare that I am married to Linda Marie Smith. I have two children: Emily Rose Smith, born June 12, 1998, and Daniel James Smith, born October 3, 2001.

ARTICLE II — DEBTS AND EXPENSES

I direct my Executor, hereinafter named, to pay all of my legally enforceable debts, funeral expenses, and the costs of administration of my estate as soon as practicable after my death.

ARTICLE III — DISPOSITION OF PROPERTY

Section 3.1. I give, devise, and bequeath my real property located at 742 Evergreen Terrace, Springfield, Illinois, to my wife, Linda Marie Smith, if she survives me by thirty (30) days. If she does not survive me, I give said property in equal shares to my children, Emily Rose Smith and Daniel James Smith.

Section 3.2. I give and bequeath all funds held in my Chase Bank checking account ending in 4521 to my wife, Linda Marie Smith, if she survives me. If she does not survive me, such funds shall be distributed equally to my children.

Section 3.3. I give and bequeath my 2019 Honda Accord, Vehicle Identification Number 1HGCV1F34KA039847, to my son, Daniel James Smith.

Section 3.4. All the rest, residue, and remainder of my estate, both real and personal, of whatsoever kind and wherever situated, I give, devise, and bequeath to my wife, Linda Marie Smith, or if she does not survive me, to my children in equal shares.

ARTICLE IV — APPOINTMENT OF EXECUTOR

I hereby nominate, constitute, and appoint my wife, Sarah Elizabeth Smith, as Executor of this my Last Will and Testament. If she is unable or unwilling to serve, I appoint my brother, Thomas Richard Smith, as alternate Executor. My Executor shall serve without bond.

ARTICLE V — POWERS OF EXECUTOR

I grant my Executor full power and authority to sell, lease, mortgage, or otherwise manage, deal with, and dispose of any and all property, real or personal, belonging to my estate, at such time, in such manner, and upon such terms and conditions as my Executor shall deem advisable, without the necessity of obtaining a court order.

ARTICLE VI — GUARDIAN

In the event that any of my children are minors at the time of my death, I nominate my sister, Patricia Ann Williams, as guardian of their persons and property.

ARTICLE VII — NO-CONTEST CLAUSE

If any beneficiary under this Will shall, directly or indirectly, contest or attack this Will or any of its provisions, any share or interest in my estate given to that beneficiary under this Will is revoked and shall be disposed of in the same manner provided herein as if that contesting beneficiary had predeceased me.

ARTICLE VIII — GENERAL PROVISIONS

This Will shall be construed and enforced in accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I have hereunto set my hand and seal this 15th day of March, 2023.
