LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT

OF

ROBERT JAMES WILLIAMS

I, Robert James Williams, of the City of Portland, County of Multnomah, State of Oregon, being of lawful age and of sound and disposing mind and memory, and under no constraint or undue influence, do make, publish, and declare this to be my Last Will and Testament, revoking all previous wills and codicils.

ARTICLE ONE — DECLARATIONS

I declare that I am unmarried, having been previously married to Diane Catherine Williams, from whom I was divorced by decree of the Multnomah County Circuit Court entered on April 7, 2018. I have three children from said marriage: William Robert Williams, born January 22, 1990; Jennifer Lynn Williams-Park, born August 14, 1993; and Steven Michael Williams, born December 1, 1996.

For the avoidance of doubt, all references to "Robert J. Williams" or "R.J. Williams" in any of my financial or property records refer to me, Robert James Williams.

ARTICLE TWO — PAYMENT OF DEBTS

I direct that all of my legally enforceable debts, including costs of my last illness and funeral, and the expenses of the administration of my estate, be paid by my Personal Representative as soon as reasonably practicable after my death, from the assets of my residuary estate.

ARTICLE THREE — SPECIFIC BEQUESTS

Section 3.1. I give and devise my condominium unit located at 1925 NW Flanders Street, Unit 4B, Portland, Oregon 97209, together with all furnishings, fixtures, and personal property contained therein, to my daughter, Jennifer Lynn Williams-Park.

Section 3.2. I give and bequeath my investment portfolio held at Fidelity Investments under account number ending in 8834, including all stocks, bonds, mutual funds, and other securities contained therein, to be divided equally among my three children: William Robert Williams, Jennifer Lynn Williams-Park, and Steven Michael Williams.

Section 3.3. I give and bequeath my 2021 Toyota RAV4, together with all accessories and equipment, to my son, Steven Michael Williams.

Section 3.4. I give and bequeath the oil painting entitled "Pacific Sunset" by Margaret Thornton, currently displayed in my residence, along with my collection of first-edition novels stored in the study, to my son, William Robert Williams.

ARTICLE FOUR — RESIDUARY ESTATE

All the rest, residue, and remainder of my estate, of whatsoever kind and nature, and wheresoever situated, including any property over which I may have a power of appointment, I give, devise, and bequeath in equal shares to my three children. If any child shall predecease me, the share of such deceased child shall pass to the surviving children in equal shares.

ARTICLE FIVE — NOMINATION OF PERSONAL REPRESENTATIVE

I nominate and appoint my daughter, Jennifer Lynn Williams-Park, as Personal Representative of my estate. She shall serve without bond. If Jennifer Lynn Williams-Park is unable or unwilling to serve, I nominate my son, William Robert Williams, as alternate Personal Representative.

My Personal Representative shall have all powers granted to personal representatives under the Oregon Revised Statutes, including but not limited to the power to sell, lease, or encumber any property of the estate, without court approval, as she deems necessary for the proper administration of my estate.

ARTICLE SIX — POUR-OVER PROVISION

Any assets of my estate not otherwise disposed of under this Will, including any assets that may have been inadvertently omitted from the Robert J. Williams Revocable Trust dated September 10, 2020, shall be added to and become part of said Trust, to be administered and distributed according to the terms thereof. Notwithstanding this pour-over provision, the specific bequests enumerated in Article Three above shall be distributed directly from my probate estate and shall not be subject to the terms of the Trust.

ARTICLE SEVEN — GENERAL PROVISIONS

Section 7.1. If any provision of this Will shall be held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

Section 7.2. This Will shall be construed and enforced in accordance with the laws of the State of Oregon.

Section 7.3. No person shall be required to furnish any bond or surety for the performance of duties as Personal Representative or in any other fiduciary capacity under this Will.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I have hereunto set my hand to this my Last Will and Testament on this fifteenth day of March, two thousand twenty-two.


______________________________
Robert James Williams, Testator
