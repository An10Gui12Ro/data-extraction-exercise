LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT

OF

EVELYN GRACE THORNTON

I, Evelyn Grace Thornton, currently residing at 2847 Pioneer Road, Lake Oswego, Oregon, being of full age and of sound and disposing mind and memory, and understanding the nature and extent of my property and the natural objects of my bounty, do hereby make, publish, and declare this instrument as and for my Last Will and Testament, hereby revoking any and all former Wills and Codicils heretofore made by me.

ARTICLE I — IDENTIFICATION AND DECLARATIONS

I declare that I am a widow. My late husband, Dr. Franklin Howard Thornton, passed away on February 3, 2019. I have two children: Catherine Anne Thornton-Reeves, born April 18, 1975, and Michael Franklin Thornton, born November 9, 1978.

ARTICLE II — PAYMENT OF DEBTS, TAXES, AND EXPENSES

I direct my Co-Executors, hereinafter named, to pay from my residuary estate all of my legally enforceable debts, costs of my last illness and funeral, expenses of administering my estate, and all estate, inheritance, and succession taxes imposed by reason of my death, whether or not such taxes are attributable to property passing under this Will.

ARTICLE III — SPECIFIC BEQUESTS OF TANGIBLE PERSONAL PROPERTY

Section 3.1. I give and bequeath my diamond and sapphire ring, originally belonging to my grandmother Lillian Hargrove, and my pearl necklace purchased in Kyoto, Japan in 1998, to my daughter, Catherine Anne Thornton-Reeves.

Section 3.2. I give and bequeath my Steinway & Sons Model B grand piano, serial number 597842, currently located at my residence, to my son, Michael Franklin Thornton.

Section 3.3. I give and bequeath my collection of antique maps, comprising approximately forty-two (42) pieces, stored in the library of my residence, to the Portland Art Museum, to be held and displayed as the institution sees fit.

ARTICLE IV — SPECIFIC BEQUESTS OF REAL PROPERTY

Section 4.1. I give and devise my primary residence located at 2847 Pioneer Road, Lake Oswego, Oregon 97035, together with all improvements, fixtures, furnishings, and personal property contained therein (except as otherwise bequeathed herein), to my daughter, Catherine Anne Thornton-Reeves.

Section 4.2. I give and devise my vacation property located at 19520 Meadow Lane, Bend, Oregon 97702, including the cabin structure and all outbuildings, to my son, Michael Franklin Thornton.

ARTICLE V — SPECIFIC BEQUESTS OF FINANCIAL ASSETS

Section 5.1. I give and bequeath all funds held in my Bank of America checking account ending in 3310 and my Bank of America savings account ending in 3317 to my daughter, Catherine Anne Thornton-Reeves.

Section 5.2. I give and bequeath my Merrill Lynch investment portfolio, account number ending in 6629, including all stocks, bonds, mutual funds, exchange-traded funds, and other securities contained therein, to be divided equally between my two children.

Section 5.3. I give and bequeath my Vanguard Individual Retirement Account (IRA), account number ending in 1104, to my son, Michael Franklin Thornton, subject to all applicable tax laws and regulations governing such distributions.

ARTICLE VI — RESIDUARY ESTATE

All the rest, residue, and remainder of my estate, of whatever kind and nature, whether real, personal, or mixed, and wherever situated, including any lapsed or failed gifts, I give, devise, and bequeath in equal shares to my children, Catherine Anne Thornton-Reeves and Michael Franklin Thornton. If either child predeceases me, the deceased child's share shall pass to his or her then-living descendants, per stirpes. If a child predeceases me leaving no descendants, the entire residuary estate shall pass to my surviving child.

ARTICLE VII — APPOINTMENT OF CO-EXECUTORS

I nominate and appoint my daughter, Catherine Anne Thornton-Reeves, and my attorney, Richard Allen Woodward, as Co-Executors of this my Last Will and Testament. Both Co-Executors shall serve without bond. If either Co-Executor is unable or unwilling to serve, the remaining nominated person shall serve as sole Executor. If both are unable or unwilling to serve, I nominate my son, Michael Franklin Thornton, as Executor.

My Co-Executors shall act by majority decision. In the event of a disagreement, the decision of Richard Allen Woodward shall control with respect to matters of estate administration and legal compliance, and the decision of Catherine Anne Thornton-Reeves shall control with respect to matters involving the distribution of tangible personal property.

ARTICLE VIII — POWERS OF EXECUTOR

My Executor or Co-Executors shall have all powers granted under applicable state law, including but not limited to the following powers exercisable without court order:

(a) To retain any property owned by me at the time of my death, for such time as my Executor deems advisable;
(b) To sell at public or private sale, lease, exchange, or otherwise dispose of any real or personal property;
(c) To invest and reinvest estate funds in any form of property or investment;
(d) To borrow money and pledge estate property as security;
(e) To employ legal counsel, accountants, investment advisors, and other professionals;
(f) To distribute estate property in cash or in kind, or partly in each;
(g) To make elections and decisions regarding tax matters.

ARTICLE IX — CHARITABLE BEQUEST

I give and bequeath the sum of Fifty Thousand Dollars ($50,000.00) to the Oregon Humane Society, Tax ID 93-0386880, to be used for its general charitable purposes.

ARTICLE X — NO-CONTEST CLAUSE

If any beneficiary under this Will, directly or indirectly, contests the validity of this Will or any of its provisions, or institutes or joins in any proceeding to contest the validity of this Will, such beneficiary's share shall be revoked in its entirety and shall be distributed as if such beneficiary had predeceased me.

ARTICLE XI — GENERAL PROVISIONS

Section 11.1. If any provision of this Will is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall not be affected thereby and shall continue in full force and effect.

Section 11.2. This Will shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Oregon.

Section 11.3. Words importing the singular number shall include the plural, and vice versa. Words importing one gender shall include the other gender.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I have hereunto set my hand to this, my Last Will and Testament, on June 1, 2024.

Evelyn Grace Thornton, Testatrix
