DECLARATION OF TRUST

THE NAKAMURA LIVING TRUST

This Declaration of Trust (hereinafter "Trust Agreement") is entered into on the date set forth on the signature page hereof by JAMES TAKESHI NAKAMURA (hereinafter referred to as "Settlor") and JAMES TAKESHI NAKAMURA (hereinafter referred to as "Trustee").

ARTICLE I — PURPOSE AND INTENT

Section 1.1. The Settlor hereby establishes the NAKAMURA LIVING TRUST for the purposes of providing for the management and disposition of the Settlor's assets during his lifetime and upon his death, avoiding the costs and delays of probate, and ensuring the orderly transition of property to the Settlor's designated beneficiaries.

Section 1.2. It is the intent of the Settlor that this Trust be revocable during the Settlor's lifetime and become irrevocable upon the Settlor's death.

ARTICLE II — TRUST PROPERTY

The Settlor hereby transfers and assigns to the Trustee, to be held, administered, and distributed in accordance with the terms of this Trust Agreement, the property listed in Schedule A attached hereto and incorporated herein by this reference. Additional property may be transferred to this Trust from time to time by the Settlor or by any other person, by inter vivos or testamentary transfer.

ARTICLE III — ADMINISTRATION DURING SETTLOR'S LIFETIME

Section 3.1. During the lifetime of the Settlor, the Trustee shall hold, manage, invest, and reinvest the trust property and shall distribute to or for the benefit of the Settlor such amounts of income and principal as the Settlor may direct from time to time.

Section 3.2. The Settlor shall have the right to reside in any real property held in this Trust.

Section 3.3. All income earned by the Trust during the Settlor's lifetime shall be taxable to the Settlor in accordance with the Internal Revenue Code.

ARTICLE IV — INCAPACITY OF SETTLOR

Section 4.1. If the Settlor becomes incapacitated, as determined by two (2) licensed physicians, the Trustee (or Successor Trustee) shall use the trust income and principal for the Settlor's health, education, maintenance, and support, in the manner to which the Settlor has been accustomed.

Section 4.2. The Trustee shall have authority to pay the Settlor's bills, manage investments, file tax returns, and take any other action deemed necessary for the Settlor's care and the preservation of trust assets.

Section 4.3. If the Settlor regains capacity, full control of the Trust shall revert to the Settlor.

ARTICLE V — REVOCATION AND AMENDMENT

Section 5.1. The Settlor reserves the right to revoke this Trust in whole or in part at any time during the Settlor's lifetime, by delivering a written instrument of revocation to the Trustee.

Section 5.2. The Settlor reserves the right to amend this Trust at any time by a written instrument signed by the Settlor and delivered to the Trustee.

Section 5.3. Upon revocation, the Trustee shall transfer and convey the trust property, or the designated portion thereof, to the Settlor free and clear of any trust.

ARTICLE VI — DISPOSITION UPON DEATH

Section 6.1. Upon the death of the Settlor, the Trustee shall pay from the trust estate all legally enforceable debts of the Settlor, costs of last illness, funeral expenses, and expenses of administration of this Trust.

Section 6.2. After the payment of the foregoing expenses, the Trustee shall distribute the remaining trust property as follows:

(a) To the Settlor's daughter, KAREN YUKI NAKAMURA-WELLS: the residential real property described in Schedule A, Item 1, together with all furnishings.

(b) To the Settlor's son, DAVID HIROSHI NAKAMURA: the commercial real property described in Schedule A, Item 2.

(c) All financial accounts described in Schedule A, Items 3 through 5, shall be divided equally between Karen Yuki Nakamura-Wells and David Hiroshi Nakamura.

(d) The Settlor's personal effects, including but not limited to jewelry, artwork, clothing, and memorabilia, shall be divided between the Settlor's children as they may agree, or if they cannot agree, as the Trustee in the Trustee's sole discretion shall determine.

ARTICLE VII — SUCCESSOR TRUSTEE

Section 7.1. In the event the Settlor is unable or unwilling to continue serving as Trustee, by reason of death, incapacity, resignation, or otherwise, the Settlor appoints KAREN YUKI NAKAMURA-WELLS as First Successor Trustee.

Section 7.2. If Karen Yuki Nakamura-Wells is unable or unwilling to serve as Trustee, HERITAGE TRUST SERVICES, INC. shall serve as Second Successor Trustee and shall be entitled to reasonable compensation for its services.

Section 7.3. Any Successor Trustee shall have all the rights, powers, duties, and obligations of the original Trustee hereunder, without the necessity of any transfer or conveyance.

ARTICLE VIII — POWERS OF TRUSTEE

The Trustee shall have the broadest powers permitted under the laws of the applicable jurisdiction, including but not limited to the following powers, exercisable without court order or approval:

(a) To retain any property transferred to this Trust without regard to diversification requirements;
(b) To sell, exchange, lease, or encumber trust property at public or private sale, for cash or on credit;
(c) To invest and reinvest in any property or securities, including mutual funds, exchange-traded funds, real estate, and partnership interests;
(d) To borrow money for trust purposes and mortgage or pledge trust property as security;
(e) To collect rents, dividends, interest, and other income;
(f) To employ accountants, attorneys, investment advisors, property managers, and other agents;
(g) To compromise, settle, or arbitrate any claims or disputes;
(h) To make repairs, improvements, and capital expenditures on trust property;
(i) To execute deeds, leases, contracts, and other instruments;
(j) To make distributions in cash or in kind;
(k) To establish reasonable reserves for anticipated expenses, taxes, and assessments;
(l) To elect and make tax-related decisions on behalf of the Trust.

ARTICLE IX — TRUSTEE COMPENSATION AND LIABILITY

Section 9.1. The Settlor, while serving as Trustee, shall not receive compensation. Any corporate or professional Successor Trustee shall be entitled to reasonable compensation.

Section 9.2. No Trustee shall be personally liable for any loss or depreciation in value of the trust estate resulting from the Trustee's good-faith exercise of powers and discretion.

ARTICLE X — SPENDTHRIFT PROVISION

No beneficiary shall have the power to anticipate, pledge, assign, or encumber his or her interest in the Trust, and no creditor of any beneficiary shall be able to reach any distribution before it is actually received by the beneficiary. This provision shall be enforceable to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law.

ARTICLE XI — PERPETUITIES AND RULE AGAINST PERPETUITIES

Notwithstanding any other provision of this Trust Agreement, every trust created hereby shall terminate no later than twenty-one (21) years after the death of the last to die of the Settlor's descendants who are living on the date this Trust Agreement is executed. Upon such termination, the Trustee shall distribute the remaining trust property outright and free of trust to the beneficiaries then entitled to receive income therefrom, in the proportions in which they are entitled to receive such income.

ARTICLE XII — GOVERNING LAW AND SITUS

This Trust Agreement and the validity, construction, and administration of the trusts created hereby shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Colorado. The situs of this Trust shall be the State of Colorado.

ARTICLE XIII — SEVERABILITY

If any provision of this Trust Agreement shall be held invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, such invalidity shall not affect any other provision of this Trust Agreement, and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

ARTICLE XIV — ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Trust Agreement, together with the Schedules attached hereto, constitutes the entire agreement between the Settlor and the Trustee with respect to the Trust created hereby and supersedes all prior oral or written agreements.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Settlor and Trustee have executed this Declaration of Trust on the 10th day of November, 2022.
